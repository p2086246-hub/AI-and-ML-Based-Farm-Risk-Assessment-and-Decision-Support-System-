AGRITECH V7 IS A CLEAN NEW-FOLDER BUILD.

Recommended process:
1. Keep your older AgriTech folder only as a backup.
2. Extract AgriTech_V7_Final_Ready_To_Run.zip into a NEW folder.
3. Do NOT merge old V1/V2/V3/V4/V5/V6 generated files into V7.
4. Run START_HERE_FIRST_TIME.bat once.
5. After V7 works, you may delete the older folders.

NEVER COPY THESE FROM AN OLD PROJECT INTO V7:
- backend/.venv/
- frontend/node_modules/
- frontend/dist/
- backend/agritech_v*.db
- backend/.env unless you intentionally need your Twilio credentials
- old App.jsx / i18n.js / styles.css
- old backend route/service files

V7 creates its own local database: backend/agritech_v7.db
